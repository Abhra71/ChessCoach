Arrows on chess.com

Open one of your finished games on chess.com and the coach shows its key moments with arrows on the board. This needs a small browser extension. It takes about a minute, once.

Fair play: the extension only shows something on games that are already finished. During a live game it shows nothing at all, and it never moves pieces.

  1. Open the extensions page
  2. Switch on "Developer mode"
    The switch is at the top right of that page.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder (paste it into the address bar at the top of the window that opens, then click "Select Folder"):
