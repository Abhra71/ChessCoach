Welcome to Chess Coach

The coach looks at your finished chess.com games, finds the habits that cost you games, and trains you out of them, one or two things at a time.

Fair play: the coach is never active during a game against a real person.

Only your public games are read (no password). Everything stays on this computer.

Starting…

The first time takes a couple of minutes. You can close this page: a notification tells you when your review is ready.

Your review is ready.

Open my review Arrows on chess.com (1 minute, optional)

From now on the coach runs quietly (icon by the clock) and checks each new game by itself.
