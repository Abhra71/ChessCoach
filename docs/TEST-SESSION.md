# Knightwise 0.5.0: guided test session (Round 1, 13 Oct)

**Done. Round 2 is docs/TEST-ROUND-2.md.** Two things below were wrong and are kept only as the record:
7.5 and 7.7 promised a review of a bot game, but chess.com doesn't share bot games with apps; and the coaches are
now two, Maya and Viktor (Zara and Arjun were removed on 13 Oct).

About 85 to 100 minutes. Do the steps in order. For each step, read **Do**, then compare with **Expect**.
If anything is different, wrong, ugly, slow or confusing, write down the **step number** and what you saw.
A short note is enough, for example: "4.3: Back button skipped two positions."

Before you start:
- Close the Knightwise Store app if it's open.
- Double-click `try.bat` in `C:\Syntax_Assembler\ChessCoach`.
- Open Chrome and log in to chess.com.
- Turn the volume up a little: the coaches talk, and moves make sounds.

## 1. Home, 10 minutes

| Step | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Look at Home when it opens | Good evening or good morning with your name. Cards slide in, numbers roll up, the small line draws itself. Zara appears at the bottom right and talks |
| 1.2 | Read Zara's bubble, then press the small minus on it | She folds into a small figure with her name. Click her: she opens again |
| 1.3 | Look at the green **Today's 5 minutes** card | Lightning icon, 7 day letters, a Start button |
| 1.4 | Look at **Your focus** | "Stop leaving pieces hanging", the habit in one line, a red pill with times and games, and 1.1 → 0.7 with "better" |
| 1.5 | Look at the 4 habit tiles | Icon, big number, short name, a green "fewer lately" or red "more lately" arrow on each |
| 1.6 | Look at **You vs you** | Green bars for your last 20 games, grey for the 20 before: 0.7 vs 1.2, 45% vs 25%, 1.4 vs 1.6 |
| 1.7 | Press **Hear your briefing** | Your focus and progress read aloud |
| 1.8 | Click the **Forks and tricks** tile | Practice opens, titled Forks and tricks, with only fork positions |

## 2. Today's 5 minutes, 10 minutes

| Step | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Go to Home, press **Start** on Today's 5 minutes | 3 dots at the top. A briefing card on the right, the board dimmed |
| 2.2 | Read the briefing | Your game against someone: date, rapid or blitz, your colour, won or lost, move number, phase. Then the situation and the **Mindset** |
| 2.3 | Press **I'm ready** | The board lights up. Arjun says your move |
| 2.4 | Make a wrong move on purpose | Low sound, the square turns red, Arjun looks worried and gives a hint |
| 2.5 | Make the right move, or press Show answer | Chime, Arjun cheers. Dot 1 turns green, or grey if you used Show answer |
| 2.6 | Press **Next**, do the puzzle | Briefing first: puzzle rating near your level, the phase, what they just played |
| 2.7 | Press **Next**, do the opening | "Your opening: the first moves of the Italian Game". You move, Arjun answers each move and explains it |
| 2.8 | Finish all 3 | Sound, confetti, Arjun says "Done for today". Back on Home the card says **Done today** and today's dot is green |
