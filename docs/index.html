Every lost game has a reason.

Knightwise is a free chess coach that sits beside you on chess.com. It finds the habit that keeps losing you games, trains you on your own positions, and puts you back at the exact move where it went wrong.

Get it free · Microsoft Store ⭐ Star on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11 · free forever · no account, no ads, no tracking · open source (GPL-3.0)

A coach, not just an engine

Stockfish decides every move. Two coaches, Maya and Viktor, explain it in plain words, and even by voice.

Your habits, found

Every move of your chess.com games is checked and sorted into the habits behind your mistakes: pieces left hanging, forks, missed mates.

Review one moment at a time

What you played, what was better, what they could have done. Labelled arrows, in the app or on chess.com's own board.

Practice from your own games

Your real positions come back after 1, 3 and 7 days until they stick, plus puzzles at your level and a gentle 5-minute daily set.

A course that follows your games

43 lessons from the basics up. A lesson counts as passed only when your games show it.

From beginner to 2,000

Five steps, each measured in your last 20 games, so you always know the one skill to work on next.

Train like a club player

Prep against any chess.com player, your opening tree, calculation, Blindfold, Guess the human move, and endgames.

Offin every game against a real person: no hints, no arrows, nothing reads the board
Rematchany game from the move where it went wrong, against a human-like opponent at their level
Your PCyour games and progress stay on your computer; nothing is sold or tracked

Ready in about a minute

No password: only your public chess.com games are read.

Install it free from the Microsoft Store.

Type your chess.com username. Your newest games are checked first, so Home opens in about a minute.

Play as usual. After each game, the coaches show what mattered and what to practise next.
